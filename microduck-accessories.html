MicroDuck Accessories

Parts, upgrades & add-ons

Genuine spare parts and upgrade modules for the MicroDuck open-source robot duck — servos, vision and IMU modules, 3D-printed shell parts, the servo control board and power accessories. All items are sourced to match the original BOM for drop-in replacement and easy customization.

01

HD1910 TTL Bus Servo

Spare or replacement metal-gear TTL bus servo (5–8.4 V, 10 kg·cm stall torque, 0.09 s/60°, 34 × 20 × 23 mm). Sold individually or in 15-pc sets.

02

IMX219 160° Camera Module

Wide-angle camera module for vision projects — drop-in replacement for the onboard camera, with the standard ribbon cable.

03

LSM6DSV16X IMU Module

6-axis inertial measurement module for orientation and motion sensing, matching the MicroDuck desktop debug configuration.

04

3D-Printed Shell Parts

Replacement structural parts printed in PLA with TPU foot pads — body shells, legs, head and mounting brackets in multiple colors.

05

Servo Control Board

Board for servo ID assignment and TTL bus configuration, plus the AMP2.0-to-5264 adapter cable for wiring.

06

Power & Cables

7.5 V DC power adapter, power cords and spare wiring for the MicroDuck — wired power, no lithium battery required.
