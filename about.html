Company Overview

Deeply engaged in embodied intelligence + AI robotics

Founded in 2021, WW-Robot (WWNT Robotics) is deeply engaged in embodied intelligence + AI education, serving vocational and applied-undergraduate institutions nationwide. Built on four self-developed hardware pillars embodied AI robots, the OpenArm open-source arm, dexterous hands, and multi-purpose training platforms we connect technology deployment, industry practice, and talent development in both directions.
