Ordering & Quotes

How do I request a quote or place an order?

Visit our Store, find your product, and click "Request a Quote" to submit an inquiry form. For bulk or custom orders, email Support@wwntAI.com with your institution name, product list, and quantity we reply within 24 hours.

Do you offer educational or startup discounts?

Yes. WWNT Robotics serves universities, labs, and small startups. Qualified institutions and early-stage teams can receive preferential pricing. Mention your affiliation when requesting a quote and we'll apply the best available rate.

What is the typical lead time after ordering?

In-stock items ship within 48 hours. Made-to-order platforms (e.g. training platforms, custom arms) typically take 2C4 weeks. You'll receive a confirmed delivery estimate with your quote.

Shipping & Logistics

What shipping methods are available?

We ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx, and SF Express for international orders, and dedicated freight for large platforms. Incoterms (EXW / FOB / DAP) are flexible confirm your preferred terms in the inquiry.

How can I track my shipment?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number. Track it anytime at jtour.ai or via the "Track Shipment" link in the top navigation.

Do you handle customs and import duties?

For DAP terms we handle export customs; import duties and local taxes are the receiver's responsibility. For DDP terms (available on request for major destinations), we arrange door-to-door clearance. Check with us for your country's options.

Pricing & Payment

What's included in the product price?

Listed prices cover the hardware, standard accessories, and basic documentation. Open-source products include full SDK access. Custom integration, training, and extended warranties are quoted separately. See Pricing for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer (T/T), PayPal, and corporate purchase orders from verified institutions. Payment terms (deposit / balance) are confirmed at order confirmation.

Technical Support

How do I get technical support?

For technical questions, email Support@wwntAI.com with your product model, serial number, and a description of the issue. We'll respond within 24 hours on weekdays.

Do you offer remote debugging or on-site support?

Yes. Remote debugging is available via video call for setup and integration issues. On-site support is available for enterprise and institutional customers in mainland China request it when opening a support ticket.

Open Source & SDK

Where can I find the open-source repositories?

Our public repos are on the Open Source page including the underlying SDK, simulation demos, and algorithm projects. Hardware is fully decoupled from software for easy customization.

Which frameworks are supported?

Our platforms integrate with ROS 2, LeRobot, Foxglove, and popular simulation environments. Each repo includes quick-start docs and example code to get you running on day one.

Products & Specs

What product categories do you offer?

We offer dexterous hands, humanoid & quadruped robots, robot arms, sensors, and training platforms from leading brands plus our own WWNT Robotics line. Browse the full catalog on the Store.

Can I customize a product for my research?

Yes. Many platforms are composable grippers, lenses, and end-effectors can be swapped. For fully custom builds, describe your task space and payload needs in your inquiry and our engineering team will propose a configuration.

Warranty & Returns

What is the warranty period?

Standard hardware warranty is 12 months from delivery, covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranties (24C36 months) are available for institutional orders. Contact us for terms.

What is your return policy?

Unopened standard products can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund (excluding shipping). Custom and made-to-order items are non-returnable unless defective. Initiate a return by emailing Support@wwntAI.com with your order number.

Can't find what you need?
