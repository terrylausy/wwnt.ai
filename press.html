WWNT ROBOTICS and UT Austin's Human-Centered Robotics Lab Announce Humanoid Robotics Collaboration

Research-grade Mini Pi Plus humanoid robots shipped to Prof. Luis Sentis's laboratory; teams to meet at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh

Guangzhou, China — September 1, 2026

WWNT ROBOTICS Corp (wwnt.ai), a robotics company headquartered in Guangzhou, China, today announced a research collaboration with the Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory (HCRL) at The University of Texas at Austin, led by Prof. Luis Sentis. Under the collaboration, WWNT ROBOTICS is supplying its Mini Pi Plus and Mini Pi Plus Pro humanoid robot platforms to HCRL for academic research, together with hardware-level technical support.

Key Highlights

  • WWNT ROBOTICS Corp is supplying its Mini Pi Plus and Mini Pi Plus Pro humanoid robots, together with hardware-level support, to Prof. Luis Sentis's Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory at UT Austin — marking the company's entry into the U.S. academic research market.

The Collaboration

WWNT ROBOTICS Corp (wwnt.ai), a robotics company headquartered in Guangzhou, China, today announced a research collaboration with the Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory (HCRL) at The University of Texas at Austin, led by Prof. Luis Sentis. Under the collaboration, WWNT ROBOTICS is supplying its Mini Pi Plus and Mini Pi Plus Pro humanoid robot platforms to HCRL for academic research, together with hardware-level technical support.

Collaboration Milestones

  • Support documentation delivered. In late August, WWNT ROBOTICS shared a complete hardware-level support documentation package with Prof. Sentis's team and its Austin-based collaborators.
  • Hardware shipped. Mini Pi Plus / Plus Pro humanoid robot units were dispatched to the laboratory and are expected to arrive in Austin in late September 2026.
  • IROS 2026 meeting confirmed. Prof. Sentis has confirmed he will visit WWNT ROBOTICS's booth at the IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 conference in Pittsburgh (September 27 – October 1, 2026).
  • On-site demo support proposed. WWNT ROBOTICS has proposed an on-site visit to the HCRL laboratory on October 2, 2026, to provide hands-on, hardware-level demonstration and support at no charge to the laboratory.

A Milestone for WWNT ROBOTICS's North America Strategy

This engagement marks a key step in WWNT ROBOTICS' North America expansion. As the exclusive North America partner of HighTorque for the Mini Pi series humanoid robots and joint modules, WWNT ROBOTICS is placing its research-grade hardware directly into leading U.S. robotics laboratories, backed by an after-sales service hub. The collaboration is academic-first: WWNT ROBOTICS supplies and supports the hardware for research use, with no commercial obligation placed on the laboratory.

About the Organizations

About WWNT ROBOTICS Corp
WWNT ROBOTICS Corp (wwnt.ai) provides research-grade humanoid robotics hardware, embodied-AI education, and university AI/robotics program enablement. The company is the exclusive North America partner of HighTorque for the Mini Pi series humanoid robots and joint modules, and the developer of OpenArm, a fully open-source humanoid arm platform built on ROS 2.

About the Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory
The Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory (HCRL) at The University of Texas at Austin, directed by Prof. Luis Sentis, focuses on whole-body control, task and motion planning, human factors, and experimental research with humanoid robots, mobile manipulators, and exoskeletons. Prof. Sentis is internationally recognized for pioneering whole-body control for humanoids and helped design and test NASA's Valkyrie humanoid robot.
